# WidgetWare Business Brief

## Overview
WidgetWare sells enterprise software and services that help manufacturing and industrial-automation companies modernize plant operations and adopt AI-enabled automation.

## Core Products & Offerings
1. **Plant Operations Platform**: Connects plant-operational information and helps teams monitor and improve manufacturing processes.
2. **Industrial AI Accelerator**: Helps industrial organizations identify and implement governed AI use cases.

## Ideal Target Profile
- **Target Industries**: Manufacturing, Industrial Automation, Automotive Manufacturing, Electronics Manufacturing, Industrial Equipment.
- **Company Size**: Large enterprises (5,000+ employees).
- **Target Buyers**: VP of Manufacturing, Plant Operations Director, Chief Digital Officer, CTO, VP of Digital Transformation, Head of Industrial AI.
- **Key Buying Signals**: New AI leadership, digital transformation programs, plant modernization initiatives, GenAI hiring, manufacturing data initiatives.

## SDR Process & Boundaries
The WidgetWare SDR process analyzes target accounts using verified context and evidence. 

### Human Approval Boundaries
All external communications and state changes require explicit human approval. Specifically:
- **No external outreach**: Sending emails or social messages is strictly prohibited without human approval.
- **No CRM updates**: Direct modifications to CRM records are strictly prohibited.
- **No financial or contractual commitments**: Pricing or legal statements must never be made autonomously.
